# Looking Back at 2025 and My Resolutions for 2026

## Context
Hey there! I'm Minh, a software developer based in Paris, originally from Vietnam. This is my first time writing a blog—actually, the first time I'm writing anything publicly. For me, this is a real "stepping out of my comfort zone" moment. The year 2025 was pretty similar—I did a ton of things I'd never done before.

## Workshops and Events I Attended in 2025

### AWS Serverless Series of Workshops (Dec 2025)

I attended 3 out of 6 Serverless workshops organized by AWS France and [Kiiro](https://kiiro.fr/) at the AWS Paris office. The final workshop happened just before the Christmas holidays and one week after [AWS Re:Invent](https://reinvent.awsevents.com/). Big thanks to the AWS France team for organizing these workshops. I got to learn about Serverless workloads, Lambda functions, EventBridge, Serverless orchestration with [Step Functions](https://amzn.to/3mHmDYy), and developing Serverless applications using [SAM](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/serverless-application-model/) and CDK.

To level up my Serverless skills, I'm building a Serverless application to scrape jobs from multiple sources: LinkedIn, Indeed, Adzuna, etc. Spoiler alert: I'm using EventBridge scheduler to run the scraping job twice a day 😋. You can check out the project [here](#/projects/2).

Lambda is a fully-managed service, which means customers can focus entirely on business logic and APIs. Meanwhile, traditional EC2 is an unmanaged service where AWS only handles "security OF the cloud" (hardware, AWS infrastructure, compute, etc.). Learn more about the AWS Shared Responsibility Model [here](https://aws.amazon.com/compliance/shared-responsibility-model/).

[Serverlessland](https://serverlessland.com/) is a website maintained by AWS where you can find tons of info about Serverless technologies. 

You can also discover all kinds of AWS workshops [here](https://aws-experience.com/emea/france/events/series/immersion-days).

### AWS Re:Invent (Dec 2025)

For me, this is the biggest Cloud event of the year. I attended online this time. Since I started learning about Cloud, Las Vegas has always been my dream destination. Hoping that one day I can attend AWS Re:Invent in person. The event kicked off with a pre-event called AWS Road to Re:Invent on Sunday the 7th. There were 10 teams on buses traveling from Los Angeles to Las Vegas. The hackathon challenge was to develop the most useless app using at least one AWS service and Kiro IDE, of course. I found this concept really fascinating—you're on a road trip and participating in a hackathon at the same time. At first, I thought I'd just stay up until 1 a.m. (yeah, 9 hours time difference between Paris and Las Vegas). Eventually, I stayed up until all 10 teams arrived in Las Vegas. The team that developed Orik (Kiro backward) won the prize. They built an application to book a flight to Vegas for the event, but you'd never actually reach Vegas. You can find out more [here](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/adam-bien_kiro-java-roadtoreinvent-activity-7401705541699538944-9OS-?utm_source=social_share_send&utm_medium=member_desktop_web&rcm=ACoAADI2edkBLdK8N_rU79ykwUcoE8WvHJthgyo) about their app. 😆

On Tuesday, I watched Mr. Matt Garman's opening keynote—he's the CEO of AWS. I was really impressed by how he presented all the new AWS services in just 10 minutes. It was like a marathon. There were many new services introduced, but the three most impressive for me were the frontier agents: DevOps agent, Security agent, and Kiro autonomous agent. The link to the AWS Re:Invent 2025 learning path is [here](https://skillbuilder.aws/learning-plan/JZQY2Z8DG4/aws-reinvent-2025-announcements-learning-plan/VWQU3VK65K). As of writing this blog, I got access denied from this course—I'll check it out later.

On Thursday, the keynote was delivered by Dr. Werner Vogels, CTO and VP of Amazon.com. This was his final keynote at AWS Re:Invent. He talked about the Dawn of the Renaissance Developer. Will developers be replaced by AI? Maybe! If you're worried, check out his keynote [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3Y1G9najGiI&pp=ygUYYXdzIHJlaW52ZW50IDIwMjUgd2VybmVy).

### Paris Java User Group (JUG) Meetup (Nov - Dec 2025)
This was my first time attending a [Paris JUG](https://www.parisjug.org/) meetup. Lucky for me, Spring Boot 4 and Spring Framework 7 had just been released. So the first meetup's presenters were Mr. [Stéphane Nicoll](https://www.linkedin.com/in/snicoll/) and Mr. [Daniel Garnier-Moiroux](https://www.linkedin.com/in/garniermoiroux/), two long-time contributors to Spring projects. In the first presentation, Stéphane covered the new features of Spring Boot 4 and Spring Framework 7, such as modularization of the Spring codebase, JSpecify, RestTemplate being deprecated (yes, you should use RestClient from now on), etc. He also introduced us to [SdkMan](https://sdkman.io/). Since then, I can't start a Java or Spring project without it.

After Stéphane's presentation, Daniel walked us through Spring Security. Honestly, security has always intimidated me. But the way Daniel presented Multi-Factor Authentication and passwordless sign-on with passkeys was genuinely fascinating and energetic. No wonder he's been voted one of the best presenters at previous [Spring IO](https://2026.springio.net/) and [Devoxx](https://www.devoxx.fr/) events.

The second JUG meetup took place one week after the first one. At this meetup, I learned more about Software Craftsmanship, presented by Mr. [Cyrille Martraire](https://www.linkedin.com/in/martraire/). I also got to meet the legendary Dr. [José Paumard](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jos%C3%A9-paumard-2458ba5/). You might know him from the Java videos by Oracle. Check out one of these videos [here](https://youtube.com/shorts/_AdrYSsFtS8?si=j2XsUNfcKoAdcfhj). Before that, I didn't know he was one of the founding members of Paris JUG.

### AWS Summit Paris 2025 (Apr 2025)
The AWS Summit in Paris is France's premier annual AWS gathering. Attending for the second time, I had the opportunity to connect with Cloud specialists from across France and internationally. While it's been 8 months and the details of various keynotes have faded, one announcement stood out: [EKS Auto Mode](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/eks/latest/userguide/automode.html). This feature revolutionizes Kubernetes cluster management by automating infrastructure operations. It seamlessly connects with core AWS services—EC2, EBS, and Elastic Load Balancing—to deliver a cloud-native experience built on AWS best practices.

## What I've Learned So Far
We can't have a year-end summary without mentioning AI. Everyone's talking about AI now 😛. 2024 was the year of studying Agentic AI. 2025 was truly a year of mass production of AI agents. We saw many companies develop agents in 2025. MCP also exploded in popularity, although it faces plenty of security challenges. GitHub even published their MCP server publicly. And yes, I'm using it right now too 😊. AI has genuinely transformed my workflow. I'll publish what I've learned about AI so far in my next blog post.

I'm also passionate about the Cloud-Native world. I've obtained three AWS certifications, two Kubernetes (K8s) certifications ([CKAD](https://training.linuxfoundation.org/certification/certified-kubernetes-application-developer-ckad/) and [CKA](https://training.linuxfoundation.org/certification/certified-kubernetes-administrator-cka/)), and even a Google GenAI certification. I'm not a Pokémon badge collector if you're asking 😄. But honestly, I didn't get any chances to work on enterprise-grade Cloud projects in 2025. However, I'm committed to continuing my Cloud training. I believe the opportunity will come to me in 2026.

I prepared for my Kubernetes certifications on [KodeKloud](https://kodekloud.com)—they have an amazing platform with hands-on labs that really helped me understand K8s deeply. For my AWS certifications, I learned from [Stéphane Maarek](https://www.udemy.com/user/stephane-maarek/) on Udemy, who is an incredible AWS teacher. His courses are so well-structured and practical. I'm also listening to a lot of videos and podcasts by [Kelsey Hightower](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kelsey-hightower-849b342b1/), one of the most famous Cloud influencers even though he retired from tech 😊.

In the final days of 2025, I've been watching the [Spring Developer](https://www.youtube.com/@SpringSourceDev) series presented by the great Mr. [Dan Vega](https://www.linkedin.com/in/danvega/). There are so many new features in Spring Boot 4 that I want to apply in my projects.
